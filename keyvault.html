🛡️ Privacy & Security Commitments

We designed EcoLith with defense-in-depth to keep your credentials strictly confidential.

Encrypted at rest: AES-256-GCM encryption with a random IV and authenticated user-binding.
Admins cannot view: Even system administrators cannot see or decrypt your API key.
Deletable anytime: Deleting your key permanently erases all ciphertext from the database.
Direct usage: Requests to Google run directly under your account limits and terms.
One Key, One User: Each API key is cryptographically bound to one account. Keys cannot converge or diverge across users.
